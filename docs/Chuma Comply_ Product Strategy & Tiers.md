# **Chuma Comply: Product Strategy & Tiers**

This document outlines the product strategy, user value, and monetization tiers for Chuma Comply. This is a non-technical guide to our business plan.

### **The Problem**

Starting a business in Zambia is complex. Entrepreneurs face a confusing maze of compliance requirements from PACRA, ZRA, NAPSA, local councils, and industry-specific laws. A simple mistake leads to costly fines and delays.

### **The Solution**

Chuma Comply is an AI co-pilot that turns weeks of legal confusion into a simple, 2-minute conversation, providing personalized, step-by-step compliance checklists.

## **Our "Freemium" Product Strategy**

Our strategy is to build trust and deliver immediate value to the widest possible audience with a **Free** tier, then upsell our most engaged users to high-value **Pro** and **Concierge** tiers.

## **Product Tiers**

### **Tier 1: Free (The General Guide)**

* **Who It's For:** All new entrepreneurs, students, and general businesses (like consultants, digital agencies, or small shops).  
* **What It Is:** An AI-powered co-pilot that provides instant, actionable checklists for **general business compliance**.  
* **Features:**  
  * Covers the "80% of compliance" that every business needs:  
    * **PACRA** (Business Name & Company Registration)  
    * **ZRA** (Tax & VAT Registration)  
    * **NAPSA** (Pension Contributions)  
    * **Employment Code** (Hiring & Contracts)  
    * **Data Protection Act** (General Data Handling)  
* **Value:** Saves users weeks of research. It's the "V1" of our project, designed to attract a large user base and prove our core technology.

### **Tier 2: Pro (The Industry Specialist)**

* **Who It's For:** Specific, high-stakes industries like **Food Service, Construction, Mining, Healthcare, or Finance.**  
* **What It Is:** An expert co-pilot with a deep knowledge base of industry-specific laws and regulations.  
* **Features:**  
  * Everything in the **Free** tier, *plus*:  
  * Access to personalized checklists based on a deep knowledge of industry-specific laws (e.g., "The Food Safety Act," "Mines and Minerals Act," "Health Professions Act").  
  * Answers the critical questions a "general" guide can't (e.g., "What are the fire safety requirements for a commercial kitchen?" or "What permits do I need for artisanal gold mining?").  
* **Value:** Prevents costly, industry-specific fines and shutdowns. This is our primary monetization tier, offering specialized, high-value advice.

### **Tier 3: Enterprise (The "The Compliance Accelerator" Agent)**

* **Who It's For:** Serious businesses that want to move from *knowing* what to do to *getting it done*.  
* **What It Is:** A full-service compliance dashboard that turns advice into action.  
* **Features:**  
  * Everything in the **Pro** tier, *plus*:  
  * **Compliance Workflows:** The app will facilitate the *actual submission* of requirements.  
  * **Form Filling:** Guided, in-app forms for common submissions (e.g., PACRA Name Reservation, ZRA TPIN Registration).  
  * **Secure Document Upload:** A vault to upload and manage required documents (e.g., director IDs, proof of address).  
  * **Status Tracking:** A dashboard to track the status of your submissions with government agencies.  
* **Value:** This tier "closes the loop." It turns Chuma Comply from an *advisor* into an *agent*, managing the entire compliance lifecycle for a business. This is our premium, high-retention offering.
